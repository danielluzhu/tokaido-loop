Day 01 · Tue

Shinagawa

Arrive From your note
Arrive

Haneda (HND) — Keikyu line runs direct to Shinagawa Station, 13 min · ¥300. Easily the best airport for this trip.Landing at Narita instead: Narita Express to Shinagawa, ~80 min · ¥3,250.

Do

Maxell Aqua Park Shinagawa — the aquarium from your list. It sits inside the Shinagawa Prince complex, two minutes from the Takanawa exit, and stays open into the evening. Dark, cool, and low-effort: the right call on a jet-lagged first day.Roughly ¥2,500. Dolphin show runs several times daily — check the board on the way in.

Stay

Shinagawa Prince Hotel — attached to the station, and the aquarium is downstairs. Four nights here, no repacking.Cheaper nearby: Sotetsu Fresa Inn Tokyo-Shinagawa.

✈
Tokyo → Fukuoka HND–FUK · 1h50 · ¥12–25k

Fly this one. The Shinkansen takes 4h50 and costs about ¥23,000 — no faster, no cheaper, and it eats your Owl Cafe morning. Haneda is 13 minutes from your hotel on the Keikyu line, which makes an early departure painless. ANA, JAL, Skymark and Peach all run the route.

Day 05 · Sat

Fukuoka

Arrive From your note
Arrive

Fukuoka Airport is unusually central — subway to Hakata Station in 5 minutes, Tenjin in 11. Drop bags, start the day.

Do

Owl cafe — the one from your list. Fukuoka's cluster around Tenjin and Daimyo; reserve ahead, since most run timed sessions and cap the room. Afternoon at Ohori Park and the Fukuoka Castle ruins next door.Evening is the real reason to come: yatai, the open-air food stalls along the Nakasu riverfront. Tonkotsu ramen at a stall, standing up, is the city's signature.

Stay

Dormy Inn Premium Hakata Canal City Mae — walkable to the yatai, and it has a rooftop bath, which you'll want after a travel day.Alternative: Mitsui Garden Hotel Fukuoka Nakasu, same area.

▮
Hakata → Shin-Osaka Nozomi · 2h30 · ≈¥15,600

Sanyo Shinkansen, straight shot. Take a morning train so Osaka gets a full afternoon — one night here is tight by design.

Day 06 · Sun

Osaka

Arrive Your blank row
Arrive

Shin-Osaka, then Midosuji line south to Namba, ~15 min. Stay on that end so the evening is on foot.

Do

One night, so pick density over coverage. Kuromon Ichiba Market in the afternoon for grilled scallops and uni, then Dotonbori once the signs come on — the Glico runner, takoyaki from a window, okonomiyaki sitting down.Osaka Castle is the trade-off you're making. ▮
Shin-Osaka → Shizuoka Hikari · ~2h10 · ≈¥10,900

Nozomi does not stop at Shizuoka. You need a Hikari — and not every Hikari stops either, so check the specific train before you board. Kodama always stops but takes closer to three hours.

Day 07 · Mon

Shizuoka

Arrive From your note
Arrive

Shizuoka Station. Sit on the left side heading east — this stretch is the classic Mt. Fuji view from the Shinkansen, weather permitting.

Do

Museum and cafe, as you wrote it. The Shizuoka Prefectural Museum of Art is the draw — its Rodin Wing holds a full cast of The Gates of Hell, and it's a genuinely strange thing to find out here.Then the cafe half: this is Japan's green tea heartland, so make it a proper tea house rather than a coffee shop. Ask for a local sencha flight.Short on time? The Shizuoka City Museum of Art is smaller and sits right in town by the station.

Stay

Hotel Associa Shizuoka — connected directly to the station, which matters with one night and an early train out.

Before you book

Skip the JR Pass

It doesn't pay off on this route. The seven-day pass runs ¥50,000 and excludes Nozomi and Mizuho without a surcharge — which is exactly what you'd want for the Hakata legs.

Add a budget flight to Fukuoka and you're still well under the pass. Buy individual tickets.

Reserve seats in advance

Use the smartEX app for Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen — it takes a foreign card and lets you change trains without a counter queue.

Your Sunday Hakata → Osaka leg is the one to lock early. Weekend westbound trains fill, and standing for 2h30 with luggage is miserable.

The Shizuoka trap

Worth repeating because it catches people: Nozomi — the fastest and most frequent Shinkansen — does not stop at Shizuoka.

Both of your Shizuoka legs need a Hikari, and some Hikari services skip it too. Confirm the individual train, not just the type.

Hotel budget

Everything above is mid-range business class, roughly ¥12,000–20,000 a night for a small, clean room near a station.

Rates swing hard by season and fill fast around Japanese holidays. Book the Fukuoka Saturday first — it's your least flexible night.

Two things to verify yourself. Fares and opening hours here are approximate and change — treat them as planning figures, not bookings. And confirm the owl cafe directly before you build Saturday around it: small animal cafes open, move, and close often, and I can't check its current status from here.
